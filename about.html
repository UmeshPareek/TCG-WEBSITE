The firm / Bengaluru

Serious about business.
Human about everything.

A Bengaluru-based business consulting firm bringing strategy, marketing, brand, operations, technology and finance together for founders and established teams.

Why TCG exists

One business.
One joined-up view.

TCG began in finance. The work kept revealing the next need: a clearer brand, a better process, a missing tool. Bringing those disciplines together became the firm’s purpose.

Today, The Consulting Group connects eight consulting practices, from business strategy and digital marketing to custom software and compliance. We work with founders and established businesses on the practical work behind a launch, a stronger operation or the next stage of growth.

A word from the founder
“We’ve built our own from zero. So we know exactly what it costs to get it wrong, and we stay until it’s right.”
Umesh Pareek / Founder

Rooted in Bengaluru, close to the businesses we work with. A firm built around practical contribution, personal involvement and the conviction that the pieces should work together.
