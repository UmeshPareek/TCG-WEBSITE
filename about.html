Who we are · Bengaluru · est. 2019

A finance brain that refused to stay in its lane.

A full-spectrum consulting firm out of Bengaluru. We take a business from a name on paper to a registered, branded, running company — one roof, one team. We're not consultants who talk. We're operators who build.

A name

on a piece of paper. That's where most of our clients start — an idea, a registration, a founder who's been quoted a fortune to make it real, by five different vendors who each own one slice and none of the outcome.

Registered. Branded. Running.

A company that actually operates. We started in finance and modelling, then kept getting pulled into the next gap — the brand, the entity, the tool, the SOP — until refusing to stay in our lane became the whole business.

Umesh Pareek · Founder
In the room, not on a retainer
