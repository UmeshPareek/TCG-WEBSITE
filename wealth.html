Business funding / your questions

Before we talk.

What does TCG Wealth help with?

TCG Wealth helps businesses explore debt funding for expansion, everyday operations and other business requirements. It is The Consulting Group’s specialist business funding segment.

Can we discuss funds for day-to-day business expenses?

Yes. Explain the expenses, the amount needed and the timing of your customer receipts. Funding for short-term operating needs is often called working capital finance. The appropriate options depend on your business and an assessment of its requirements.

What should I share in a business funding enquiry?

Start with your name, company, sector, business stage, approximate funding amount and purpose. You do not need to upload financial documents here. If confidentiality matters, request an NDA before sharing detailed information.

Can TCG help with a financial model or pitch deck too?

Yes. Our finance practice prepares financial models, management reporting and pitch decks. This is a separate consulting scope from arranging business funding. Explore financial modelling and pitch deck services.

Does an enquiry confirm funding or loan approval?

No. An enquiry starts a conversation. Funding availability, eligibility, approval, rates and repayment terms require separate assessment and confirmation. The website does not promise a loan or a funding amount.

Is TCG Wealth for personal investments?

TCG Wealth focuses on funding for businesses. This page does not offer personal investment management, mutual funds or personal loans.
