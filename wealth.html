What is TCG Wealth?

TCG Wealth is TCG’s business funding segment. We help businesses explore debt-based financing for their requirements.

What should I share in an initial enquiry?

Your business name, sector, stage and a brief description of the funding need are a useful start. The initial enquiry does not ask for financial documents.

Are terms or funding approval confirmed through this website?

No. An enquiry starts a conversation. Specific availability, eligibility and terms would need to be discussed and confirmed separately.
